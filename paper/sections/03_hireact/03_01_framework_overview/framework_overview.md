## 3.1 Problem Definition and Framework Overview

Given an actor motion sequence, human reaction generation aims to predict the corresponding reactor motion sequence. The central challenge of this task lies in simultaneously modeling two complementary aspects of realistic reactions: global motion plausibility with interaction-consistent semantics, and fine-grained hand-centric interaction details, especially hand-target contact and local interaction geometry. These objectives are difficult to model jointly within a single holistic generator.

Based on this observation, we decompose the task into two complementary sub-problems. The first is initial reaction generation, which produces a globally plausible and interaction-consistent initial reaction sequence from the actor motion and provides a stable motion basis for subsequent local contact modeling. The second is contact-aware local refinement, which operates on contact-relevant temporal windows of the initial reaction and further improves hand-target contact accuracy and local interaction geometry. This decomposition separates global reaction generation from local contact refinement and assigns them to two different modeling levels.

Built on this formulation, we propose HiReact, a two-stage framework for human reaction generation. Stage1 extracts multi-scale interaction cues from the actor motion and generates an initial reaction through body-hand dual reaction generation. On the actor side, HiReact combines global and local body-part representations to model interaction intent at multiple spatial scales. On the reactor side, it explicitly models body and hand responses with separate streams to preserve fine-grained hand motion details. Stage2 further refines the Stage1 output by first locating contact-relevant windows and then performing contact-aware residual refinement with hand-region semantics, coarse contact state, and local geometry cues. The refined local windows are finally aggregated into the refined full-sequence reaction. Through this hierarchical design, HiReact jointly improves global reaction quality and local contact accuracy.
