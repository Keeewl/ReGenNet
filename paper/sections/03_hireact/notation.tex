\paragraph{Notation.}
Let $Y=\{y_t\}_{t=1}^{T}$ denote the actor motion sequence and $X=\{x_t\}_{t=1}^{T}$ denote the ground-truth reactor motion sequence, where $T$ is the sequence length and $x_t, y_t$ represent the motion states at frame $t$. We use $\tilde{X}=\{\tilde{x}_t\}_{t=1}^{T}$ to denote the initial reaction sequence generated by Stage~1. In our implementation, motion is represented as a joint-channel-time tensor, i.e., $X, Y, \tilde{X}\in\mathbb{R}^{J\times C\times T}$, where $J$ is the number of joints and $C$ is the number of motion channels per joint.

For structured interaction modeling in Stage~1, we decompose the reactor motion into body and hand components, denoted by $X_b$ and $X_h$, respectively, and similarly use $\tilde{X}_b$ and $\tilde{X}_h$ for the corresponding Stage~1 predictions. On the actor side, we denote the global motion representation by $Y_g$ and the $i$-th local part representation by $Y_{p_i}$, where $i\in\mathcal{P}=\{1,\dots,P\}$ and $P=6$ in our setting. The Stage~1 generator is thus formulated as $\tilde{X}=G(Y)$, or equivalently as $(\tilde{X}_b,\tilde{X}_h)=G(Y_g,\{Y_{p_i}\}_{i=1}^{P})$ when emphasizing the multi-scale actor conditioning and body-hand dual reaction generation.

For Stage~2, we perform local refinement on contact-relevant windows. We use $Y^{(m)}$, $\tilde{X}^{(m)}$, and $\hat{X}^{(m)}$ to denote the actor motion, initial reaction, and refined reaction of the $m$-th window, respectively, and optionally use $X^{(m)}$ for the corresponding ground-truth reactor window. The refiner predicts a residual motion $\Delta X^{(m)}$ and updates the initial reaction as
\[
\hat{X}^{(m)} = \tilde{X}^{(m)} + \Delta X^{(m)}.
\]
Each window is associated with a selected hand $s^{(m)}$, a primary target region $q^{(m)}$, and a top-$K$ target-region set $\mathcal{Q}^{(m)}$. We denote the coarse contact state by a contact mask $M^{(m)}$ and a distance map $D^{(m)}$. The local geometry cues are denoted by $\Delta^{(m)}$, $d^{(m)}$, and $v^{(m)}$, corresponding to relative position, relative distance, and distance velocity, respectively. We use $Z^{(m)}$ to denote the overall contact-aware conditioning of the $m$-th window, which aggregates the hand-region semantics, coarse contact state, and local geometry cues. The Stage~2 refiner is thus written as
\[
\Delta X^{(m)} = R\big(Y^{(m)}, \tilde{X}^{(m)}, Z^{(m)}\big).
\]
