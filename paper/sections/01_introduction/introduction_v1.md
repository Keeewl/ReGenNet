Human reaction generation aims to synthesize a plausible response motion for one person conditioned on the motion of another interacting person. As a core problem in human-human interaction modeling, it is important for interactive animation, virtual humans, and behavior simulation. Recent methods have made meaningful progress in generating globally reasonable and temporally coherent reaction motions. However, in realistic interaction scenarios, global plausibility alone is not sufficient. What ultimately determines interaction realism is often fine-grained local behavior: subtle hand and upper-body responses, part-specific reaction patterns, and accurate local contact geometry. Motivated by this gap, we present **HiReact**, a hierarchical interaction-aware framework for fine-grained human reaction generation. HiReact is designed to preserve global reaction plausibility while improving fine-grained local interaction quality, especially hand-centric contact realism.

The key challenge is that fine-grained reaction generation involves multiple coupled difficulties. First, existing holistic generators primarily model overall motion plausibility, but often fail to preserve fine-grained local responses of the reactor, especially hand-centric reactions, whose subtle variations are easily smoothed out by full-body generation. Second, high-quality reaction generation depends not only on the actor's global motion pattern, but also on localized interaction cues conveyed by different actor body parts. In other words, the actor should not be treated merely as a coarse motion condition; instead, it should provide structured interaction evidence at multiple spatial scales. Third, even when the overall reaction appears reasonable, local hand-target contact and interaction geometry can still be inaccurate. A sequence may look globally plausible while remaining locally imprecise in the very regions that are most critical for interaction realism. Taken together, these observations suggest that fine-grained reaction generation requires jointly modeling localized interaction intent, preserving hand-centric local responses, and improving local contact accuracy, which is difficult to achieve within a single holistic generator.

To address these challenges, we propose **HiReact**, a hierarchical two-stage framework that decouples globally plausible reaction generation from fine-grained contact refinement. In **Stage 1**, we design a multi-scale interaction-aware generator with asymmetric roles for the two participants. On the actor side, we construct **Global + Multi-Part conditions** to encode interaction cues from multiple spatial scales, enabling the model to capture localized intent expressed by different body parts. On the reactor side, we perform **body-hand dual reaction generation**, explicitly separating hand-centric response modeling from broader body motion generation so that fine-grained local responses are less likely to be washed out by holistic motion synthesis. In **Stage 2**, we further improve the generated reaction through **local contact-aware refinement**. Instead of regenerating the entire sequence, Stage 2 focuses on contact-relevant local regions and windows, refining the initial reaction to improve hand-centric interaction accuracy and local contact fidelity. This design allows HiReact to combine robust global generation with targeted local refinement for the most challenging interaction details.

Overall, HiReact provides a coherent hierarchical solution for fine-grained human reaction generation by combining multi-scale interaction-aware initial reaction generation with targeted hand-centric contact refinement.

Our contributions can be summarized as follows: (1) We present **HiReact**, a hierarchical interaction-aware framework for fine-grained human reaction generation. (2) We propose a multi-scale **Stage 1** generator that models actor interaction intent and reactor response asymmetrically through Global + Multi-Part actor conditioning and body-hand dual reactor generation. (3) We propose a hand-centric, contact-aware **Stage 2** refinement module that improves local interaction accuracy and contact fidelity beyond globally plausible reaction generation.
